# Preface and General Provisions

[D00-P1] Welcome to Haida Co. The company is a manufacturing business and values safe production, product quality, honesty and integrity, and employee growth. This handbook explains onboarding, work discipline, pay and leave, social insurance, resignation and related matters, sets out the rights and obligations of the company and its employees, and serves as the basis for day-to-day management and for handling the related procedures.
[D00-P2] Employees should read this handbook carefully and consult the Administration & HR Department promptly if they have questions. The company shall provide the necessary job guidance, working conditions and safety training, and shall respect employees' dignity and lawful rights and interests.

## General Provisions

[D00-G1] This handbook applies to employees who have an employment relationship with the company. For dispatched (agency) workers, on-site safety, confidentiality and workplace order are managed by reference to this handbook in accordance with the law, while matters of their employment relationship are handled under the law and the relevant agreements; interns, re-hired retirees and similar personnel are managed under their agreements and applicable law.
[D00-G2] This handbook is formulated on the basis of national labour laws and regulations and the applicable rules of Jiangsu Province and of the city where the company is located. No clause may reduce statutory rights; more favourable treatment lawfully agreed in an employment contract or collective contract continues to be honoured.
[D00-G3] In this handbook, "the company" means Haida Co.; "the Administration & HR Department" is the department that carries out personnel management duties, and the personnel functions of the Personnel Department, the General Office and the General Affairs Office named in earlier documents are all covered by this term. The direct supervisor is responsible for day-to-day arrangements, the department head for managing the department, and the General Manager or a person authorised by the General Manager in writing for the corresponding approvals.
[D00-G4] Rules that bear on employees' vital interests, such as remuneration, working hours, rest and leave, safety and health, insurance and benefits, training and work discipline, shall go through the procedures required by law, including discussion by employees and consultation on an equal footing with the trade union or employee representatives, and shall be publicised or made known to employees. A signed acknowledgement of receipt shall not be treated as an employee's waiver of statutory rights or as a substitute for the democratic procedure.
[D00-G5] This handbook and its supporting operating procedures together constitute the company's rules. Supporting documents must not conflict with this handbook or with laws and regulations; where a change to the employment contract is involved, it shall be handled according to law. Employees have the right to raise issues through internal grievance channels or statutory channels, and the company must not retaliate.
