# Chapter 6 — Pay

## 1 Payment of wages

[D06-1.1] The company pays wages on the basis of the employment contract, the lawfully established pay system and the work actually performed. The components of pay, the method of calculation and the pay day shall be stated in the employment contract or in a written pay confirmation; for monthly pay the pay cycle does not exceed one month, and wages must not be withheld without reason.
[D06-1.2] Where the pay day falls on a statutory public holiday or a rest day, wages are paid in advance according to law. The company gives employees a payslip itemising gross pay, overtime pay, lawful deductions and net pay, and employees may check it.
[D06-1.3] Probation pay must not be lower than the standard set by law. Where an employee performs normal work, the wage must not be lower than the applicable minimum wage; the minimum wage, overtime pay and pay for each type of leave are each calculated under their own rules.
[D06-1.4] Where a monthly salary has to be converted into a daily or hourly wage, the statutory 21.75 paid days per month and the corresponding number of hours are used; 26 days is not used as a uniform statutory conversion standard. Piece-rate pay, performance pay and commission are calculated under lawful rules that have been made known, and this clause does not replace the lawful determination of the specific wage base.

## 2 Absence and benefits

[D06-2.1] For personal leave, or for absence verified to be without a proper reason, only the pay for the time actually not worked, for which no wage is payable by law, is not paid; there is no deduction at a multiple, no additional fine, and no deduction of sales commission already earned or other remuneration already accrued.
[D06-2.2] Statutory paid leave, sick leave, the paid work-injury leave period and similar are paid under their own rules. In payroll, the same period of absence must not be deducted twice, and statutory paid time must not be counted as unpaid absence.
[D06-2.3] The existing full-attendance bonus of 100 yuan per month is retained. Eligible employees receive this bonus each month; an employee who takes personal leave or has verified absence without leave in a month does not receive it for that month. As under the original rules, up to two occasional late arrivals a month of no more than 5 minutes each do not affect the full-attendance bonus. Leave that by law counts as attendance, the company's two days of paid sick leave per month, off-site work on company business and a verified missed clock-in do not affect the bonus.
[D06-2.4] For other approved sick leave, this handbook does not separately provide for cancelling the full-attendance bonus; sick pay payable by law and the full-attendance bonus shall be calculated so that nothing is counted twice. Wages payable by law must not be cut in a disguised way under the name of adjusting the full-attendance bonus or similar.

## 3 Deductions and compensation

[D06-3.1] The company does not impose punitive fines for lateness, leave, breaches of discipline or the like, and does not withhold all wages in place of disciplinary action. Items lawfully withheld, such as individual income tax and the employee's own share of social insurance and housing fund contributions, shall be shown on the payslip.
[D06-3.2] Where an employee causes a loss, the actual loss, fault and causation shall be established by investigation and the matter handled by negotiation according to law; any deduction of compensation from wages must have a lawful basis and comply with the statutory proportion, wage protection and procedural limits. Disputes are resolved through statutory channels, and no arbitrary deduction is made first.
[D06-3.3] The bonus system is established according to law and made known in advance; a wage-type bonus that has been agreed or whose payment conditions have been met must not be cancelled by a unilateral ad hoc notice. Changes to the normal wage standard are handled according to law.
