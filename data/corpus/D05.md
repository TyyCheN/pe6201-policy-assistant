# Chapter 5 — Performance Appraisal

[D05-1] Performance appraisal is used to give feedback on work performance, improve skills and set performance incentives reasonably. Appraisal shall be objective, fair and verifiable, combining work results, quality, safety, ability and teamwork.
[D05-2] Before the appraisal period begins, the department shall explain to employees the indicators, weights, period, scoring basis and the link to performance pay; where a major change to the pay system is involved, the corresponding procedure is carried out according to law, and the standards of a period that has already been completed are not changed retroactively.
[D05-3] Routine appraisal is combined with annual appraisal: the direct supervisor proposes the evaluation, the department head reviews it, and the Administration & HR Department files it. Employees have the right to see their own appraisal result and its basis, and to submit a written explanation or a request for review.
[D05-4] The grades are: A for 90 points and above; B for 80 points and above but below 90; C for 70 points and above but below 80; D for 60 points and above but below 70; E for below 60 points. The ranges do not overlap.
[D05-5] Grades A and B may be taken into account for rewards, promotion or development; for grades D and E, the matters to be improved shall be made clear. Bonuses, awards and promotion follow the conditions announced in advance, and leave taken according to law, work injury or the performance of statutory obligations must not be used as a reason for an unfavourable evaluation.
[D05-6] Grade E does not by itself amount to being not competent for the job in the legal sense. Where an employee is in fact not competent, the employee shall be re-assessed after training or a lawful change of position; only if the employee is still not competent and the statutory conditions are met may the contract be terminated according to law, with the obligations of notice, severance pay and review of special protections fulfilled.
[D05-7] An employee may request an internal review within 5 working days of receiving the appraisal result. The Administration & HR Department shall organise the review and reply within 10 working days of receiving the request; where an extension is needed for objective reasons, the reasons shall be given. The internal time limits do not restrict the employee's statutory remedies.
