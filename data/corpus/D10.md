# Chapter 10 — Supplementary Provisions

[D10-1] The Administration & HR Department is responsible for the day-to-day interpretation of this handbook, training on it and keeping its records. Interpretation shall be consistent with the text, the employment contract and the law, and must not be used to add penalties, reduce wages or benefits, or widen the grounds for termination.
[D10-2] This handbook must complete the democratic rule-making procedure according to law, be approved by the company and be publicised or made known to employees, and it takes effect on the date stated in the formal release notice. The revision date on the cover only identifies the version and does not mean that the democratic procedure has been completed or that the handbook is already in force.
[D10-3] Once this handbook formally takes effect, it replaces the corresponding clauses on employee labour management in the original Employee Handbook and in the Rules and Regulations dated 28 April 2025; the old documents no longer serve as a second set of standards for the same matters. Matters that arose before it takes effect are handled under the rules lawfully in force at the time, and liability is not increased retroactively.
[D10-4] Other valid special operating procedures continue to apply to the extent that they do not conflict with this handbook and the law. The employment contract, the collective contract and more favourable treatment already lawfully established are preserved according to law; where an adjustment is needed, it is separately agreed according to law or put through the statutory procedure.
[D10-5] Later revisions that bear on employees' vital interests are discussed, consulted on and publicised or made known under the statutory procedure, with records kept of the version, the effective date and delivery. Merely signing for receipt does not mean that the employee accepts that all clauses are lawful, and is not treated as a waiver of the right to object or to seek a remedy.
[D10-6] The Administration & HR Department shall provide employees with a paper copy or an electronic version that can be consulted and downloaded. The electronic and paper versions have the same content; if a difference between versions appears, the formal release record shall be checked and the difference corrected promptly, and an unidentified version must not be used to an employee's disadvantage.
[D10-7] In this handbook, "working day", except in clauses that concern a specific shift schedule or where the law gives it another meaning, means a working day on which the company handles the corresponding administrative procedures; required attendance days are determined by the employee's lawful shift schedule. A calendar month, calendar quarter and calendar year are counted by the Gregorian calendar. Where laws or regulations make new provision, that provision applies and the handbook is revised promptly.
