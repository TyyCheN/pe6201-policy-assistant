# Chapter 8 — Insurance

[D08-1] The company enrols employees in social insurance according to law; its obligation to enrol and contribute runs from the date the employee starts work, and registration is completed within the statutory time limit. Employees on probation are enrolled in the same way, and statutory contributions are not replaced by waiting for confirmation, by the employee voluntarily giving them up, or by a cash subsidy. The housing fund is handled under the applicable rules.
[D08-2] The company declares the contribution base and pays contributions on time as required; the part the individual bears by law is withheld from wages and itemised. Employees shall provide the true information needed for the procedures, and the company must not pass the employer's share on to the individual.
[D08-3] Medical expenses are handled under the rules for basic medical insurance, maternity insurance and work-injury insurance. Commercial insurance cannot replace social insurance; the liability the company bears by law for failing to enrol an employee, for work injury and for other matters is not removed because the employee has insurance.
[D08-4] Where an accident or occupational disease that may be a work injury occurs, treatment shall be given promptly, the matter reported and evidence preserved; the company applies for a work-injury determination within the time limit set by law, and the right of the employee and close relatives to apply according to law is not restricted.
[D08-5] The company, according to law, informs employees of occupational disease hazards, provides protection and monitoring and carries out occupational health examinations, keeps records and protects health information; the employment contract of a person who has not had the pre-departure occupational health examination required by law must not be unlawfully terminated or ended.
