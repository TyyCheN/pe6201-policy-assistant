# Chapter 4 — Training

[D04-1] The company provides pre-job, safety, business, skills and job-change training so that employees understand their duties, the risks and the operating requirements. Training is organised by the Administration & HR Department or the employee's own department, which keeps the sign-in, content and assessment records.
[D04-2] Employees shall attend the training arranged by the company as required. Training time, costs and travel are handled under the law and the company's relevant rules; the cost of necessary training and labour protection that the company should bear must not be passed on to employees.
[D04-3] For an employee who is not competent for the job, the supervisor shall explain the specific gaps, provide reasonable guidance, training or a lawful change of position, and tell the employee the improvement targets, the support measures and the deadline for re-assessment. An employee must not be found incompetent on subjective impression alone.
[D04-4] Where the company pays for special training and gives an employee professional technical training, it may lawfully conclude a separate service-period agreement. The penalty for breach must not exceed the actual training cost, and must not exceed the cost attributable to the part of the service period not yet served; no penalty for leaving is charged on this basis for ordinary onboarding, safety or routine job training.
[D04-5] After obtaining a qualification, completion certificate or further-study certificate related to the position, an employee may submit a copy to the Administration & HR Department for the record, as a reference for job development and selection; originals are not retained.
