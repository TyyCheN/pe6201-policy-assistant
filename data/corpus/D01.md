# Chapter 1 — Onboarding

## 1 Personal information

[D01-1.1] When reporting for duty, a new employee shall provide true and valid documents that are directly related to establishing and performing the employment relationship, such as identity, education, qualifications, work history and proof of leaving the previous employer. Originals are returned immediately after verification; the company must not retain identity documents, collect a deposit or require a guarantor.
[D01-1.2] If an employee's contact address, telephone number, emergency contact or job-related qualifications change, the employee shall inform the Administration & HR Department within 30 days; if the change affects emergency contact or work arrangements, the employee shall inform the department promptly.
[D01-1.3] The company processes employees' personal information only to the extent necessary for recruitment, labour management and statutory obligations, and takes the necessary protective measures for sensitive information such as health and financial account information. Employee records may be used only by authorised personnel and must not be circulated casually.
[D01-1.4] The company may lawfully verify important documents that are directly related to the position. Where there are doubts about the documents, the employee shall be given an opportunity to explain or correct them; whether the matter constitutes fraud or grounds for termination is determined on the evidence and according to law, and the contract is not terminated directly because of an ordinary form-filling error.

## 2 Reporting for duty

[D01-2.1] The employee reports for duty as stated in the offer notice and completes registration, signing of the employment contract, collection of the company rules, job assignment and onboarding training. An employee who cannot report on time shall contact the Administration & HR Department in advance to make arrangements.
[D01-2.2] The employment relationship is established from the date the employee starts work. The company shall sign a written employment contract in good time according to law and give the employee a copy of the contract. Job duties, hiring conditions, wage standard, place of work, working-hours system, occupational hazards and similar matters shall be disclosed truthfully.
[D01-2.3] On joining, the employee collects the necessary work supplies and protective equipment, and an issue record is kept. For special operations or positions that by law require a certificate, the employee must hold the relevant qualification and be authorised for the post before starting work.

## 3 Onboarding guidance

[D01-3.1] The company assigns each new employee an onboarding mentor, who introduces the department's functions, its people, job duties, safe operation, use of equipment, attendance, leave requests and the expense reimbursement process. New employees may consult the mentor or the Administration & HR Department.
[D01-3.2] An employee who has not completed the necessary safety training and passed the assessment must not independently operate hazardous equipment such as stamping presses, lifting equipment or electrical equipment, or carry out the corresponding work.

## 4 Probation and confirmation

[D01-4.1] A probation period must be agreed in the employment contract. The company may agree only one probation period with the same employee. For a contract term of three months or more but less than one year, probation must not exceed one month; for one year or more but less than three years, it must not exceed two months; for a fixed term of three years or more, or an open-ended contract, it must not exceed six months. No probation period may be agreed for a contract whose term is the completion of a specific task or whose term is less than three months.
[D01-4.2] Hiring conditions and assessment standards shall be made clear to the employee at the time of hiring or at the start of probation. The assessment shall relate to the requirements of the position and rely on verifiable records of work results, ability and behaviour; no discriminatory condition unrelated to the position may be set.
[D01-4.3] The department head shall complete the assessment before the probation period expires and pass the result to the Administration & HR Department for processing. Probation must not be extended unilaterally, pay must not continue at the probation rate, and a second probation must not be agreed, on the ground that the internal confirmation approval has not been completed.
[D01-4.4] Termination of the employment contract during probation must have a statutory basis. Where the ground is failure to meet the hiring conditions, there must be hiring conditions that were made known to the employee and corresponding evidence, and the decision must be made within the probation period according to law, with reasons given; receiving any single disciplinary action must not by itself be treated as failure to meet the hiring conditions.

## 5 Contract management

[D01-5.1] The Administration & HR Department handles the conclusion, renewal, amendment, termination and expiry of employment contracts and keeps the records. Matters that need to be changed by agreement shall be confirmed in writing by both parties, each holding one copy.
[D01-5.2] Work assignments and transfers shall be necessary for the business and reasonable, taking into account what the employment contract provides, job skills, working conditions, health and pay; the company must not use an insulting or punitive transfer to force an employee to resign.
